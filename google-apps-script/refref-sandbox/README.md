`CreditLedger.gs` is an owner-only, append-only Google Sheet ledger for earned, redeemed, released, and reversed Jonezie Cash. Its source was saved in the private Apps Script project and verified against this file. The owner reviewed and granted the account-wide Google Sheets permission on November 10, 2026. The first synthetic run created the private **Jonezie Cash Ledger - Private** sheet and completed successfully; its four rows and zero ending balance were verified in Google Sheets. Do not make a public web-app deployment for this ledger. Run the ledger tests with `node --test scripts/test-jonezie-credit-ledger.cjs` after changes.

`PaidInvoice.gs` was saved in the private Apps Script project. It prepares a RefRef purchase event only after the Square invoice is marked fully paid and the owner supplies the verified Square customer ID, invoice ID, paid amounts, and the customer's paid-order number. The paid-history builder requires all prior paid Square invoice IDs through the current invoice. Its reward-to-credit validator was installed and exercised against one real RefRef Sandbox reward on November 10. It requires the reward's project, event, partner, status, delivery method, currency, and unadjusted amount to match verified owner facts. RefRef may accept a purchase while its reward evaluation is pending; do not grant Jonezie Cash until an actual earned reward is confirmed. Run `node --test scripts/test-refref-paid-invoice.cjs` to verify the payload rules.

`LedgerSmokeTest.gs` is installed in the private project for one synthetic grant, redemption, cancellation release, and refund reversal cycle. It completed on November 10, 2026 with a 2000-cent grant, 1200-cent redemption, 1200-cent release, 2000-cent grant reversal, and 0 cents available. All four rows were inspected in the private ledger sheet. This verifies ledger mechanics with synthetic data, not paid-invoice integration or a live partner balance.

`RefRefSandboxFlow.gs` records a verified paid purchase and submits complete paid history on the first review for a customer. Subsequent invoices use the verified paid order number and the earlier accepted history. Before issuing credit, the bridge checks the 7% base amount and, on a first order, the 13% bonus. It uses the reward ID to prevent duplicate ledger entries and marks the reward fulfilled after recording the credit.

The paid invoice, RefRef, and owner review scripts are installed in the private Apps Script sandbox. On November 10, 2026, the sample review awarded $20 on a $100 first paid order and $3.50 on a $50 repeat paid order. The unpaid sample generated no RefRef event or credit. Repeating the first approval preserved its existing $20 credit. The first attempt exposed an HTTP 404 on a second history upload. The successful retest used the previously accepted history for the repeat purchase. No real Square invoice was created for this test.

`OwnerPaidReview.gs` runs against the **Paid Reviews** tab in the private Jonezie Cash ledger. Check full payment, prior paid invoices, and partner code pairing. Enter one row in USD, with invoice IDs in chronological order and the current invoice last. Set the three check columns to `YES`, enter `APPROVE` in Action, and run `processJoneziePaidReviewsSandbox()`. For `PENDING_REFREF` or `PARTIAL_REFREF`, enter `RECHECK` and rerun the function. It uses the stored event ID. A direct recheck also requires the verified paid order number and commissionable merchandise in cents. Correct a `CHECK_INPUT` row before retrying. Investigate `NEEDS_REVIEW` before further submission.

Customer-facing partner codes may use the first initial plus full surname (for example `ZLEONARD`). Use `node scripts/generate-affiliate-alias.cjs Zack Leonard` to suggest an available alias; a duplicate name gets `2`, `3`, and so on. RefRef still generates a separate tracking code. After approving a partner, pair the two in `affiliate-codes.json`, for example `{"activeCodes":[{"code":"ZLEONARD","refCode":"REFREF_CODE_FROM_PARTNER_DASHBOARD"}]}`. The site sends both codes to the owner; paid-invoice events must use the RefRef `refCode`, not the customer-facing alias. Keep `activeCodes` empty until launch. The RefRef Partner app continues to show its generated code; named vanity links require RefRef's paid Starter plan.
